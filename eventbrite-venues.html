UX design

Eventbrite Venues

A venues discovery and booking platform designed for Eventbrite: one place to compare spaces, check availability and get in touch with organizers, instead of piecing information together from Instagram, Google Maps and a dozen open tabs.

The problem

Comparing venues for an event meant keeping many different pages open at once. Information was scattered across social media, maps and individual websites, rarely presented in a way that made it easy to compare capacity, price or availability at a glance.

Two users, one platform

Research with venue owners and event organizers pointed to two different needs behind the same problem: organizers wanted a fast, visual way to filter and compare spaces, while venue owners wanted to get discovered beyond word of mouth and Instagram, without answering the same questions over and over by WhatsApp.

Three rounds with Eventbrite

The wireframes went through three review sessions with the Eventbrite team. The first version was judged too generic, so it was rebuilt around two clear flows, finding a venue and listing a venue, instead of trying to solve for every possible user. The next round refined the venue pages themselves: past and upcoming events for context, a clear distinction between featured and trending listings, and separate ratings for attendees and organizers. The last pass focused on interaction details and visual consistency.

Result

A four-step listing flow for venue owners, a searchable and filterable directory for organizers, and a venue profile that brings pricing, availability and reviews together on one page.
